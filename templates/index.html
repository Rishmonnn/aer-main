Terms & Conditions and Data Privacy Policy

Data Privacy Notice

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), AERIS — the Academic Evaluation, Records & Information Systems of the College of Engineering and Architecture — is committed to protecting the privacy of your personal information.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your full name, PHINMAed email address, role (Program Head or Faculty), and CEA department. This information is used solely for academic evaluation, records management, and system administration purposes.

How We Use Your Data

  • To verify your identity and role within the College of Engineering and Architecture
  • To provide access to academic records and evaluation tools
  • To facilitate communication related to academic processes
  • To generate reports and analytics for institutional improvement

Data Protection

Your personal data is stored securely and is accessible only to authorized personnel. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, and request the deletion of your personal data. For concerns regarding your data privacy, please contact the CEA Data Privacy Officer.

Consent

By creating an account, you consent to the collection, use, and processing of your personal information as described in this policy.
